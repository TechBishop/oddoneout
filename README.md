# Odd One Out

A fast-paced word association game where three words share a secret connection, and one does not belong!

## How to Play
- Spot the connection and tap the odd word out before the timer expires.
- Earn Sparks to unlock cosmetic themes and topic packs.
- Complete the 5-round Daily Target to build your daily streak.
- Watch rewarded ads to earn 2X Sparks on your next round or revive your streak on game over.
