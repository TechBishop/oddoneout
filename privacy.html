Privacy Policy for Odd One Out

Last updated: October 28, 2026

1. Introduction

Odd One Out ("we", "our", or "the Game") is a mobile and web word puzzle game. We are committed to respecting and protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding user data.

2. Data Collection and Usage

We do not require you to create an account, register, or provide personal identity information (such as your real name, email, or phone number) to play Odd One Out.

  • Game Progress & Settings: Your high scores, best streaks, unlocked cosmetics, and gameplay preferences are stored locally on your device via HTML5 LocalStorage. This data stays on your device and is not transmitted to our servers.
  • Advertising (Google AdMob / AdSense): Odd One Out uses third-party advertising services provided by Google (Google AdMob and Google AdSense). These services may collect and process device identifiers (such as the Android Advertising ID or Apple IDFA), IP addresses, and interaction metrics to serve relevant, non-personalized or personalized video and banner ads.

4. Children's Privacy

Odd One Out includes optional adult and mature humor topic packs that are age-gated. If you are under the age required in your jurisdiction, you should play only standard, family-friendly categories. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13.

5. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updates will be posted directly to this URL.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions or feedback regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact the developer via our official store listing or developer portal.
